Location: Starsector 3D test range
Date: Milestone 2

Flight sandbox for the Starsector 3D mod. Deploy the flagship, then the 3D view takes over.

Mouse steers (reticle). Left click fires the selected weapon group (1-7 switch groups; autofire groups shoot on their own). The enemy nearest your reticle gets a lead circle: put the reticle in it (it turns green) and the green + gun pippers show where your shots land. R locks a target. B toggles an incoming-fire test (enemy missile launchers shoot at you; your PD lasers shoot their missiles down). N repairs and rearms everything, you included.

W/S throttle, X all stop, A/D strafe, Space/C strafe up/down, Q/E roll, L auto-level, hold Left Alt (Option) to free-look, T tactical view, mouse wheel zoom, [ and ] switch hull, P pause, H help.

K reloads data/config/s3d_tuning.json. O hides the 3D view (debug). Esc opens the normal menu to exit.

The parked lineup and the circling drone are targets: they take damage and only shoot back with missiles when B is on. Projectiles, beams and missiles all work; shields, flux and fighters come later.
